What We Do

Detailing done properly — every panel, every seam.

From a quick refresh to a full paint correction, we tailor every job to your vehicle's condition and your goals.

Exterior Hand Wash

Two-bucket hand wash, pH-neutral soap, iron and tar removal, and a gentle dry that leaves zero swirls.

Interior Deep Clean

Full vacuum, steam extraction, carpet and upholstery shampoo, leather conditioning, and vent detailing.

Paint Correction

Machine polishing that removes swirl marks, oxidation and light scratches for deep, glossy clarity.

Ceramic Coating

Professional-grade ceramic protection that repels water, blocks UV, and makes every future wash easier.

Engine Bay Detail

Careful degrease, rinse and dressing of the engine bay — clean enough to show off, safe for electronics.

Headlight Restoration

Sand, polish and seal yellowed headlights back to crystal clarity — safer at night, sharper looking.

About Us

Hi — we're Pat & Princess.

We started detailing out of a simple frustration: too many "car washes" that left swirl marks, wet carpets and a lingering smell. We wanted to do it the right way — by hand, with the right products, and with enough time to actually do the job properly.

Princess leads interiors. If there's a stain, a smell, or a crumb hiding in a seat rail, she will find it. Pat handles the paint — decontamination, machine polishing and ceramic coatings that hold up to real-world weather.

Every vehicle gets the same care whether it's a ten-year-old commuter or a weekend showpiece. We're fully insured, we use pH-neutral and eco-conscious products, and we treat your car like it's parked in our own driveway.
